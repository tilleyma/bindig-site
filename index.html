Four processes

Each one runs on your machine against your Rekordbox export. Nothing is written until you approve it.

PROC_01

Fix tags

Download-site junk, artist names in title fields, misspelt artists, blank genres. Every fix rated by confidence and approved by you. Online lookups (Discogs, MusicBrainz) coming next.

PROC_02

Build sets

Playlists ordered by Camelot key, BPM and energy, built from the tracks you actually play, one per core genre.

Find gaps

Finds the releases you own only part of and play, with shop links to complete them.

Purge

AI reads what you actually play and scores every track for fit. Suggested cuts arrive grouped by genre, so you review one shelf at a time. Nothing is deleted.
