Privacy

Last updated 29 September 2026 · BINDIG is in beta

In short: your audio and file paths never leave your computer. Your library details are used to build your results and then thrown away, unless you choose to share them with us during the beta. We never sell or share your data.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, including any library you shared during the beta. Email bindig.dj@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.
