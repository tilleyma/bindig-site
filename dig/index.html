Let's dig your tracks.

BINDIG reads your crate, then digs out the music you should own but don't.

1Reads plays, labels, dates and keys across your whole collection.
2Writes your DJ profile: who you are behind the decks, and where you're heading.
3Digs a crate of ten tracks you don't own yet, in the lane you're playing now.
Drop your rekordbox.xml or choose the file
  1. Open rekordbox on your computer.
  2. File → Export Collection in xml format.
  3. Save it anywhere, then drop it above.
Your file stays on your device. Only a short summary (top artists, labels, genres, BPM) goes to the AI to write your profile and dig your crate.
